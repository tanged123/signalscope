Data conversion
Each continuous preset retains all 256 upstream samples. RGB channels are
rounded to the nearest 8-bit integer, then packed as consecutive six-digit
hex values in contour-presets.json. SignalScope linearly interpolates these
sRGB samples. Gray interpolates black and white. All data ships offline.
